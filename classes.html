Four classes · eight people each

Cooking classes you can book in Sydney

Every class is hands-on from the first minute. You get your own bench, your own knife and your own pan, and you eat what you cook at the end of it.

Knife Skills Basics

Three hours · eight people · AUD 95

The class most people should take first. We cover how to hold a knife, how to keep it sharp, and the four cuts — slice, dice, julienne and chiffonade — that most recipes actually ask for. You will get through a lot of onions.

Knife Skills Basics in detail

Bread Baking Fundamentals

Five hours · eight people · AUD 150

A starter you take home, a loaf you shape yourself, and an honest explanation of why the last loaf you baked came out dense. Includes a proofing basket and a written schedule you can follow at home.

Bread Baking Fundamentals in detail

Pasta From Scratch

Three hours · eight people · AUD 120

Egg dough made by hand — no machine needed at home — rolled into two shapes, then a sauce built in the same pan the pasta finishes in. We also cover what to do when the dough tears, because it will.

Pasta From Scratch in detail

Saturday Market Table

One day · eight people · AUD 240

We meet at the market in the morning with a shopping list and a budget, decide the menu from what is actually good that day, then cook it as a shared table. The most useful class if you want to stop cooking from recipes.

Saturday Market Table in detail
