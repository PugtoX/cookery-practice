Booking terms

The short version: tell us early if you cannot come, and tell us about allergies when you book rather than when you arrive.

Cancelling or moving a date

More than 7 days before
Full refund, or move to any other date
2 – 7 days before
Move to another date, or a credit valid for 12 months
Less than 48 hours
No refund; you are welcome to send someone in your place

Tell us as early as you can. A late cancellation usually means an empty bench that somebody else wanted.

If we cancel

If we cancel a class — because a teacher is ill, or too few people booked — you choose between a full refund and a place on any other date. We will tell you as soon as we know rather than on the day if we can avoid it.

Allergies and dietary requirements

Tell us when you book, not when you arrive. We can work around most requirements given notice, because we buy for the class in advance.

Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten, dairy, eggs and shellfish. We can keep your food separate and clean down, but we cannot promise a kitchen free of any of them.

In the kitchen

You will be using sharp knives and a hot stove. Closed shoes are required; we provide aprons and everything else. Please follow the teacher’s instructions on knives and heat.

You are welcome to take photographs in class. Ask before photographing other people.
