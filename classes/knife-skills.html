Knife skills class in Sydney

Three hours on the one skill that makes every other recipe faster. You will use your own knife, on your own board, and leave with a system rather than a party trick.

Who this class is for

Complete beginners and people who have cooked for years but were never taught. If you currently avoid recipes that start with "dice an onion", this is the class to take before any of the others.

What we cover

  • How to hold a knife so your wrist stops aching after twenty minutes.
  • A sharpening routine you can repeat at home, with the stones you already own.
  • The four cuts that cover most recipes: slice, dice, julienne and chiffonade.
  • Which vegetables punish a dull knife, and how to tell before you start.
  • Board setup, bench scrapers, and keeping your workspace clear while you work.

You will get through a lot of onions, carrots and cabbage. Everything you cut goes into a soup we eat at the end.
