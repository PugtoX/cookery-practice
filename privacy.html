Privacy policy

This page explains what we do with the details you send us. It is short because we collect very little.

What we collect

If you use the callback form, we receive the name, the email address or phone number, the class you are asking about and any notes you add. If you email or call us instead, we receive whatever you choose to send.

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies on this site, and we do not track you across other sites.

What we do with it

We use your details to answer your enquiry and, if you book, to run the class you booked. That is all.

We do not sell your details, and we do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not turn into a booking are deleted within twelve months. Booking records are kept for seven years because tax law requires it.

Asking us to delete it

Email hello@example.com and ask. We will delete what we hold and confirm that we have done it, unless the law requires us to keep a booking record.
