About

We teach cooking the way it is learned at home — by doing it, badly at first, with someone standing next to you who can see what went wrong.

Every class is hands-on and ends with a sit-down meal. Nothing is deep-fried, and no class needs equipment you do not already have at home.

How a class runs

Four steps

  1. You book a date and tell us about allergies or anything you do not eat.
  2. You arrive fifteen minutes early — apron, knife and bench are already set up.
  3. We cook together for the length of the class, tasting as we go.
  4. You eat what you made, and take the recipe card home.
